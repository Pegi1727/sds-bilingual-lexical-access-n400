# 🧪 Methodology & Experimental Protocol

This document provides the full methodological specification for the study: **"From L1-Mediated Translation to Direct L2 Conceptualization: A 6-Month Longitudinal ERP (N400) and Behavioral Investigation of the Semantic-Direct-System (S-D-S)"**.

---

## 1. Study Design & Participants

### 1.1 Design Framework
A 6-month prospective, longitudinal, randomized controlled intervention trial with three fixed measurement timepoints:
* **Session 1 (M1):** Baseline assessment (prior to intervention).
* **Session 2 (M3):** Mid-intervention assessment (after 3 months / 36 sessions).
* **Session 3 (M6):** Post-intervention endpoint assessment (after 6 months / 72 sessions).

### 1.2 Cohort Characteristics
* **Sample Size:** $N = 40$ adult Persian-speaking learners of English as a Foreign Language (EFL).
* **Allocation:** Stratified block randomization into two parallel arms:
  * **Experimental Group (EG, $n = 20$):** Semantic-Direct-System (S-D-S) pedagogical framework.
  * **Active Control Group (CG, $n = 20$):** Standard Communicative Language Teaching (CLT) curriculum.
* **Inclusion Criteria:** Right-handed (assessed via Edinburgh Handedness Inventory); normal or corrected-to-normal vision; no history of neurological or psychiatric conditions; baseline intermediate English proficiency (CEFR B1).
